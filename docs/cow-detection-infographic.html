Step 2 · Detect

The AI finds the cow

Each picture goes to YOLO11n (“You Only Look Once”), a small, fast AI model that scans the whole picture in one pass and returns boxes, names and scores.

Model
YOLO11n yolo11n.pt (80 objects)
Bigger option
YOLOv8 Open Images, 600 objects
Confidence
≥ 50 % (you can change this on the web page)

AFixing AI mix-ups

From some angles the model may call a cow a “dog”. The --treat-as dog=cow setting counts those as cows, so none are missed.

dog cow renamed before any check

BNo double counting

After renaming, one animal may get two boxes (one from “cow”, one from “dog”). When two cow boxes overlap by 60 % or more, only the most confident one is kept.

Step 3 · Road check

Is the cow on the road?

No zones to draw: asphalt is recognised by how it looks, so it still works if the camera moves. Each pixel (on a fast 320-px copy) is road only if it passes all four tests:

① Grey?

Almost no colour: red, green and blue nearly equal.

✓ asphalt
✗ grass, soil, sky
② Mid-bright?

Brightness 15–200 (of 255): not black, not too light.

✓ asphalt
✗ shadows, white walls
③ Grainy?

Rough texture (≥ 2.2 % variation), like asphalt.

✓ asphalt
✗ metal, glass, walls
④ Wide & big?

Thin strips and patches under 2 % are removed.

✓ the road
✗ rails, kerbs, tyres

The “feet band” rule

The app checks a strip around the bottom edge of the cow’s box, where the hooves are. The cow’s own pixels are left out, so a black cow is never mistaken for asphalt.

Cow on the road: 72 % road in the band
Cow on the verge: 12 % road in the band
│ = 30 % line. At or above it the cow is on the road.

Tip: turn on “Show detected road” on the web page to see it tinted blue.

Step 4 · Warn

Four ways you are warned

Only a cow on the road triggers the warnings. Each one has its own cooldown, so you are told quickly but never flooded.

Flashing red box

On the live web page the box grows bigger and flashes red with a glow, labelled in capitals: COW 87%.

Blinks every 0.35 sEnglish / Malay labels

Voice warning

The computer says it out loud: “Alert! Cow detected.”

Repeats at most every 10 smacOS voice

Telegram message

A photo is sent straight away, then a 5-second video (2 s before the alert and 3 s after).

Once per 60 s per object/photo · /status · /on · /off

ESP32 light & buzzer

A warning light (or relay) and a buzzer on the road side blink and beep together while the cow is on the road.

Keeps going 3 s after it leavesUSB · 115200 baud

How the ESP32 light stays safe

The app sends one short message over USB every 0.5 s: ON (blink) or OFF.

Off at startThe light is off when the board powers up.
Fail-safeNo message for 3 s (app closed, cable pulled) and it switches off.
Self-findingThe app finds the board’s USB port by itself.
